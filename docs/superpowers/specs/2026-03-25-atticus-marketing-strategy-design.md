## 1. Market Context

### Total Addressable Market (Ontario)

| Segment | Count |
|---|---|
| Solo practitioners | ~8,200+ |
| Small firms (2–10 lawyers) | ~2,678 |
| **Total target firms** | **~10,880** |

- 73% of Ontario law firms are solo — the largest, most underserved segment
- 71% already use cloud-based practice management (high tech comfort)
- 18% of solos now use AI tools — fastest-growing adoption segment
- Solo firms are increasing tech spend at 2x the industry average growth rate
- Monthly software spend comfort zone: $49–$179 CAD/month

### Competitive Gap Atticus Fills

| Product | Price (CAD/mo) | Trust Accounting | AI | CRM | Ontario-Specific |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| uLaw | $49–$64 | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| Clio Essentials | $129 | No | No | No | Partial |
| Clio Essentials + Grow | $218+ | No | No | Yes | Partial |
| CosmoLex | $145–$175 | Yes | No | No | Partial |
| LEAP | ~$200+ (opaque) | Yes | Yes (LawY) | Partial | Partial |
| **Atticus** | **$149** | **In development (2026)** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes — built for Ontario** |

No other product in the Ontario market combines AI-native intelligence + CRM + Ontario-specific features + Ontario-specific features (Limitation Period Calculator, LSO Rule 3.3, PIPEDA, By-Law 9) in a single product under $150 CAD/month. This is a genuine, uncontested position.

### Key Competitor Insights

- **Clio** ($400M ARR, 400K users): Dominant in Canada but expensive and fragmented — trust accounting requires a separate QuickBooks subscription; CRM is a $218+/month stack. Their user base is increasingly frustrated with cost.
- **PCLaw** (Thomson Reuters): Effectively dead — no new features, server-based, Windows-only. Thousands of Ontario lawyers are actively migrating off it. This is a hot acquisition opportunity with zero competition.
- **CosmoLex**: Has the CBA exclusive partnership but zero AI, zero CRM. Strong compliance angle, weak innovation.
- **uLaw**: The low-cost Ontario incumbent at $49–$64/month. Dated UX, no AI, no CRM. Perceived as "good enough" by the least tech-savvy solos — not Atticus's initial market.
- **Harvey AI, Lexis+ AI**: Enterprise-only (25+ seat minimums, $30K–$300K+ contracts). Not competitors for solo/small market.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

For the first 10 users, be narrow. Expand only after proving the model.

**Target profile:**

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Firm type | Solo practitioner (not 2–10 person firms — solo first) |
| Location | Ontario, GTA/Toronto area |
| Practice areas | Family Law, Real Estate, or Wills & Estates |
| Behavior signal | Actively evaluating a tool switch, or expressed frustration with current software in the last 12 months |
| Current tool | Clio (frustrated by cost) or spreadsheets |
| Tech posture | Tech-comfortable, willing to try AI tools |

**Why these 3 practice areas:**
- Family law: High deadline density, court dates, limitation periods — acute pain
- Real estate: High-volume, repetitive admin, closing deadlines everywhere
- Wills & Estates: Trust account–heavy, compliance anxiety, often solo

**Secondary ICP — PCLaw migrants:**
- Lawyers still on PCLaw are a separate, valuable segment with a different value prop: migration simplicity, not AI productivity
- This segment skews older and less rate-focused, so the pitch is "get off a dead product easily" not "recover 23 hours"
- Use a distinct outreach sequence for them (see Channel 4)

**Why GTA/Toronto first:**
- Highest density of Ontario solos
- Easier to meet in person for demos
- Most likely early adopters of tech

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Current Pricing: $149 CAD/month

Well-positioned — above the dated uLaw tier ($49–$64), below the Clio full-feature stack ($218+). Competitive with CosmoLex ($145–$175 CAD) while being meaningfully better on AI and CRM.

### Founding Member Offer (Recommended for First 10 Users)

- **$99 CAD/month, locked for life**, for the first 25 customers
- Display on pricing page: "Founding Member spots: [X] remaining"
- Creates urgency, rewards early adopters, and turns them into vocal advocates
- This is the only time-limited pricing lever needed at this stage

### Long-Term Pricing Structure (post-10-user milestone)

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $169 CAD/month | Full access |
| Annual | $149 CAD/month ($1,788/year) | Saves $252/year; improves cash flow |
| Founding Member | $99/month (locked) | First 25 customers only |

**Hard floor: Never go below $99/month.** Lawyers equate price with quality. Being perceived as cheap kills conversion with the $300–$500/hr billing tier.

### ROI Framing (use everywhere)

> "At $400/hr, recovering just 4 hours/month covers Atticus more than 10x over. Most users report recovering far more."

*(Do not use the "$9,200 / 6,160% ROI" framing — it implies Atticus recovers all 23 admin hours, which is an overclaim lawyers will push back on in demos. The conservative version is more credible and still compelling.)*

## 6. Channel Strategy

### Prioritized by ROI for First 10 Users

| Priority | Channel | Cost | Time to First User |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold outreach via Atticus (use the product on itself) | $0 | Week 3–4 |
| 2 | Beta user referrals | $0 | Week 1–2 |
| 3 | LinkedIn warm outreach | $0 | Week 4–6 |
| 4 | PCLaw migration targeting | $0 | Week 4–6 |
| 5 | Limitation Period Calculator (free tool / SEO) | $0 | Week 5–8 |
| 6 | FOLA county law association newsletters | $0–$500 | Week 6–8 |
| 7 | OBA CPD/section content sponsorship | $0–$2K | Week 8–12 |
| 8 | practicePRO/LAWPRO listing | $0 | Week 8–12 |
| 9 | LSO Solo & Small Firm Conference (exhibit) | $2K–$8K | Annual event |
| 10 | LinkedIn ads | $500–$2K/month | Only after organic validates messaging |

### Channel Details

**1. Cold Outreach via Atticus (Highest Leverage)**
- Use Atticus's built-in LSO directory scraper to pull 500–1,000 solo Ontario lawyers
- Filter: Family Law, Real Estate, Wills & Estates, GTA/Toronto area
- Build 3 sequences (one per practice area) in Atticus itself
- 3-step sequence: Email Day 1 → LinkedIn Day 4 → Email Day 8
- Positive reply auto-moves to `consultation_booked` (already built)
- This channel proves the product works on its own target customer — powerful for investor/press stories

**CASL Compliance Note:** Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) governs commercial electronic messages. Cold emails to LSO directory contacts do not establish implied consent by default. To comply:
  - Every email must include a clear, easy unsubscribe mechanism (already built into Atticus's outreach system)
  - Use the "legitimate interest" basis: document that you are contacting legal professionals about a tool specifically designed for their regulated profession and clearly relevant to their practice
  - Keep the unsubscribe list (`emailUnsubscribes` table) current and honored within 10 business days
  - Do not email anyone who has previously unsubscribed
  - Consider legal review of the CASL Section 6(1) business-to-business implied consent provisions before sending at scale

**2. Beta User Referrals**
- Ask every beta user for one warm introduction to a lawyer friend
- Offer: 1 free month for every paying referral
- These users are the cheapest, fastest path to users 1–3

**3. LinkedIn Warm Outreach**
- Connect with 10–15 Ontario solo lawyers/day with a personal note
- Note: "I saw you practice family law in Mississauga — building practice management software for Ontario solos and would love to hear what tools you use."
- Research first, pitch second — lawyers respond to being seen as an individual, not a contact
- Follow up with Atticus pitch 3 days after connection accepted

**4. PCLaw Migration Targeting**
- Search LinkedIn for "PCLaw" + Ontario lawyers
- DM: "I noticed you're on PCLaw — we built a migration tool for Ontario solos moving off it. Happy to share what we built."
- Zero competition in this window; PCLaw users are actively looking for a replacement

**5. Ontario Limitation Period Calculator**
- Already live at `/calculator` — optimize it
- Meta title: "Ontario Limitation Period Calculator | Free Tool for Ontario Lawyers" (no year — keeps it evergreen; update annually if year is added)
- Add email capture: "Get the Ontario Limitation Period Cheat Sheet PDF" → email drip → trial offer
- Submit to LSO Technology Resource Centre for a resource listing
- This is the most scalable organic lead magnet you have

**6. FOLA County Association Newsletters**
- Target: Toronto Lawyers' Association, Peel Law Association (Mississauga), Durham Law Association
- Contact the president or newsletter coordinator directly
- Offer a short sponsored product mention or a 300-word article on Ontario AI compliance
- Cost: $0–$500 per association; reaches 100–300 solos who are your exact ICP

  *Efficiency shortcut:* The **County and District Law Presidents' Association (CDLPA)** coordinates all 46 FOLA county associations and holds an annual meeting. A single CDLPA introduction could unlock newsletter access to all county associations simultaneously — more leveraged than approaching each one individually.

  *Zero-cost community channel:* The **OBA Sole, Small Firm & General Practice section** has active online communities (LinkedIn groups, Facebook groups). Organic, non-promotional participation — answering questions about limitation periods, AI compliance, trust accounting — builds credibility and name recognition with the exact ICP at no cost. Participate before pitching.

**7. OBA CPD/Section Content**
- Join OBA or contact the Sole, Small Firm & General Practice section chair
- Offer a 30-minute CPD talk: "AI for Ontario Solo Lawyers: What's Compliant, What's Risky, What's Worth It"
- Positions Atticus as an educator — lawyers prefer this over pure sales
- This talk can be repurposed as a YouTube video, blog post, and email sequence

**8. practicePRO / LAWPRO Partnership**
- practicePRO is LAWPRO's risk management arm — it actively promotes tools to reduce malpractice
- Pitch: "Atticus reduces the two most common Ontario solo malpractice risks: missed limitation periods and trust accounting errors."
- A practicePRO listing or mention is the highest-credibility signal available for Ontario lawyers
- LAWPRO recorded 3,758 malpractice claims in 2024 — deadline/trust errors are a major category

**9. LSO Solo & Small Firm Conference**
- The single best annual event for this ICP — 20+ editions, LSO-hosted, directly for your target market
- Invest once you have 5+ paying users and real testimonials
- An exhibit booth + sponsored session (demo slot) is the format

**10. LinkedIn Ads (Later)**
- Only activate after cold outreach validates messaging and ICP
- Target: "Ontario" + "lawyer" + "sole practitioner" + Family/Real Estate/W&E
- Use the hook copy: "Your first briefing arrives tomorrow at 9am."
- Send to /calculator or a dedicated trial landing page (not homepage)

## 8. Demo Script (20-Minute Structure)

1. **The Hook (2 min):** Show the morning briefing email — "This is what your 9am looks like."
2. **The Problem (3 min):** "How many hours last week went to data entry and deadline chasing?"
3. **Upload a document (4 min):** Drop in a sample contract or voice note. Watch AI extract entities in real time.
4. **Chat demo (4 min):** Ask "What are the deadlines across all my cases?" → RAG answer with sources.
5. **Limitation Period Calculator (2 min):** Show the free public tool. "This alone saves lawyers hours every week."
6. **Pipeline / prospects (2 min):** Show the kanban view, AI win strategy.
7. **Pricing + CTA (3 min):** Show Founding Member offer. "Spots are limited — lock this in today."

**⚠️ Demo script note:** Trust accounting (By-Law 9, three-way reconciliation, trust ledger) is on the product roadmap but not yet fully built. Do NOT demo trust accounting features or show them in the competitive comparison during demos until the feature is live. If a prospect asks, the honest answer is: "Trust accounting integration is on our near-term roadmap. The current product focuses on deadline management, AI document intelligence, and client pipeline — which is where most solos lose the most time."

**Post-demo sequence:**
- Same day: "Great talking — here's your free trial link. Your first briefing tomorrow."
- Day 3 (if no sign-up): "Any questions about getting set up?"
- Day 7: "Sharing a case study from a Toronto family lawyer who caught a limitation period in week one."

## 13. Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Lawyers slow to switch from incumbent tools | White-glove onboarding + data import tool removes friction |
| Clio adds AI-native features aggressively | Ontario-specific compliance is a moat Clio can't easily copy |
| Low cold email response rates | A/B test 5 subject lines in first 100 sends; iterate fast |
| Trust accounting not yet fully built | Do not claim it in demos or marketing materials until live. Communicate it as a near-term roadmap item. It is table stakes for Ontario by Year 1 — prioritize building it. |
| Lawyers wary of AI and data privacy | Lead with PIPEDA, Canadian data residency, LSO Rule 3.3 on every touchpoint |
| CBA endorsement locked by CosmoLex | Target OBA sections, FOLA, and practicePRO instead — comparable reach, less competition |
